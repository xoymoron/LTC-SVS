What changes across versions?

Original SoulX uses the pretrained SoulX-Singer. No label uses the adapted model with no technique request. Guidance ×4 and ×8 apply the selected ON or OFF command to the target words. Score, singer reference and noise seed are matched within each example.

About the experiment

We trained technique embeddings and the score encoder on 5,415 English and Chinese GTSinger clips while keeping the flow decoder frozen. The evaluation covered 3,310 target regions from two held-out singers. These three excerpts are the listening examples used in the capstone presentation.
